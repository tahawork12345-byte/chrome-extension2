Atlas New Tab – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Atlas New Tab ("the extension") replaces Chrome's new tab page with shortcuts, search, live wallpapers, weather, a media player widget and an optional AI assistant. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide creditworthiness or lending.

1. Data stored only on your device

These settings are saved in your browser with chrome.storage, localStorage and IndexedDB. They are never sent to us:

  • Your workspaces, shortcut cards and links
  • Appearance settings, theme, cursor choice and chosen search engine
  • Wallpaper choice and any wallpaper file you upload
  • A city name you enter for weather

Uninstalling the extension or choosing "Reset to defaults" deletes this data.

2. Media playback information

To show a "now playing" widget with play, pause and skip controls, the extension reads media details from open tabs that play audio or video, such as YouTube or Spotify. These details are the track title, artist, artwork, playback position and the tab's site. This information is processed only inside your browser, kept in temporary session storage, and deleted when the browser closes. It is never sent to any server.

3. Data sent to third-party services

FeatureData sentSent to
AI assistant (optional, only when you send a message) The messages in your current conversation Our assistant server (hosted on Vercel), which forwards them to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Our server does not store or log conversations. Google handles the data under the Gemini API Terms.
Weather Your approximate location (latitude/longitude, only if you allow location access) or the city name you type Open-Meteo for the forecast and BigDataCloud for the place name
Search The text you search for The search engine you picked, such as Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave or YouTube. Your browser opens it directly.

Don't type sensitive personal information into the assistant.

4. Permissions

  • storage: saves your settings and layout on your device.
  • scripting and access to websites: detect media playing in your tabs for the now-playing widget, send play/pause/skip commands to it, and apply your chosen custom cursor on web pages. The extension does not read, collect or send any other page content, form input or browsing history.

5. What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, browsing history, analytics or tracking data. The extension has no user accounts and does not use cookies for tracking.

6. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.
